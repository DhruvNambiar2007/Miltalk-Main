About the product

A few practical questions.

What is Miltalk?

Miltalk is a set of handheld learning kits that students train with their own examples. They choose two categories, collect data and test the kit’s predictions.

What is the difference between the two kits?

The Vision Kit has a camera and learns from pictures. The Audio Kit has a microphone and learns from sounds.

Who is it designed for?

Our activities are designed for students aged 12 to 15 in schools and community groups.

What are the simulation projects?

They are small working models that connect a kit’s prediction to an action. Our servo motor recycling sorter is one project currently in development.

How can our school try Miltalk?

Send us an enquiry with your year group, student numbers and available time. We will discuss the activities, kits, facilitation and pricing for your session.
