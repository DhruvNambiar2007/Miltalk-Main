What is Miltalk?

Miltalk is a handheld AI learning kit that children teach using their own examples. The Vision Kit has a camera for pictures. The Audio Kit has a microphone for sounds. Children choose two categories, collect examples and teach the kit to tell them apart.

Why we started Miltalk

Children encounter AI in their apps, classrooms and everyday lives. We started Miltalk to help them understand how a machine learns from data. By teaching a kit themselves, they can see how their examples shape its answers and why those answers need checking.

Why teach classification?

Classification means predicting which category something belongs to. It is one of the tasks used in AI. Learning it gives children a concrete way to investigate training data, recognise mistakes and test whether a model works on something new.

Learn with pictures and sounds

How it works

Children teach classification by doing it. For example, they label pictures as recyclable or non-recyclable, then show the kit a new object. If its prediction is wrong, they look at the training examples, add better ones and test again.

1

Train

Give the kit examples for two categories.

2

Test

Try an example it has not seen before.

3

Investigate

Notice the wrong result and ask which examples led to it.

4

Improve

Add or change examples, then test again.
